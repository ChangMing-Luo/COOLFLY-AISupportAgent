This message appears when the feeder is still linked to a previous owner's account — common with second-hand or returned units. Ask the previous owner to remove the device in their app (Devices → Settings → Remove device). If you cannot reach the previous owner, contact support with your order number and the device SN; our team will verify the purchase and unbind it within 2 business days.
